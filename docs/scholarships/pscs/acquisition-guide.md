# Acquisition guidance for the three priority documents

The built-in CORE Academic college renewal guide includes acquisition steps in
“How to prepare” for Certificate of Registration (COR), Barangay Certificate of
Residency, and School ID or any Valid-ID. Issuing-office information appears
immediately after those steps, before document checking.

## Barangay location reference

The user supplied `Pinagbuhatan-Barangay-Hall.pdf` on 13 November 2026. It identifies
Pinagbuhatan Barangay Hall, includes a Google Maps place link with coordinates
14.5571675, 121.0909313, and states working hours of 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.

These location details apply to Pinagbuhatan residents. Other residents are
directed to their own barangay hall. The reference does not state service days,
fees, processing times, or required supporting documents; the guide asks applicants
to confirm those details with their barangay office before visiting.

## School process references

General school guidance was checked against these official examples on
13 November 2026:

- [FEU Registrar Services](https://www.feu.edu.ph/registrar-services/) describes
  generating a COR through the student portal after enrollment is confirmed.
- [Sulu State University enrollment process](https://sulustatecollege.edu.ph/index.php/enrollment-process/)
  directs students to the registrar for COR and Student Affairs for ID validation.

These are examples of school processes, not instructions to visit those schools.
Applicants are directed to their own registrar, official portal, or designated ID
office and asked to confirm document, photo, fee, and release requirements there.
This guidance does not add scholarship eligibility or acceptance rules; the
selected cycle’s official source list still governs those questions.
