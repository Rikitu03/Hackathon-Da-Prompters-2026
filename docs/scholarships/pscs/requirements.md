# Pasig City scholarship documentary requirements

Research date: **13 November 2026, Asia/Manila**. Prepared for GabayIskolar, especially Member 4's OCR and source-grounded RAG tests.

This is a research reference for an independent prototype. A document list belongs to a particular program, applicant classification, school year, and sometimes semester. A verified historical list does not establish the requirements or availability of a later application cycle.

## Scope and evidence

The main coverage is the **Regular Scholarship Program (Academic), SY 2025–2026**, and **CORE (Academic) renewal, SY 2026–2027**. These are the names used in the respective announcements; preserve them when recording sources. PAG-ASA has a separate college checklist below.

The requested [Pasig City Scholarship Office Facebook page](https://web.facebook.com/PasigCityScholarshipOffice) could not be read directly during research. Its announcements were checked through Pasig City's official website and the office's [AGOS Telegram channel](https://t.me/pasigscholarship), which the city website explicitly links. Official infographic attachments were visually inspected, and the linked 2026 renewal forms were downloaded and read. This is not a complete inspection of the Facebook timeline or comments.

## Shared document sets

Use these set IDs in the grade-level sections and demo bundles. Each row describes a document category, not the number of files to upload.

### N25 — new Regular Academic applicants, SY 2025–2026

| Document | Detail to preserve in the demo | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Enrollment evidence | Identify the applicant, school, and relevant school year | S25-REQ |
| Most recent report card | Preserve the grade period and student identity | S25-REQ |
| Household income evidence | Select the applicable employment branch from the table below | S25-REQ |
| Barangay residency certificate | Include the length of residence | S25-REQ |
| Valid identification copy | Make the identifying text readable | S25-REQ |
| 1 × 1 photograph | White background | S25-REQ |
| Completed, printed application | Include signatures; use the correct applicant classification | S25-PROCESS, S25-SIGN |
| Essay | At most 300 words; discuss the motivation for applying and intended contribution to the community | S25-NEW |
| Interview intake form | Separate from the application; [officially linked blank intake form](https://bit.ly/PCS-INTERVIEW_INTAKE_FORM) | S25-NEW |

The 2025 process also includes a short interview for new applicants. That is a process step, not another uploaded document. [Source: S25-PROCESS](https://t.me/pasigscholarship/337?single).

### Income evidence branches

The [2025 checklist](https://t.me/pasigscholarship/335?single) and [2026 renewal graphic](https://admin2022.pasigcity.gov.ph/storage/news/2026/08/20//6a86663c2c729_773167147-1029879349939140-4259349032637178597-n.jpeg) distinguish the following:

| Household member's situation | Listed evidence | Useful fictional sample |
|---|---|---|
| Employed | Latest ITR or latest one-month payslip | Payslip with employer, period, and amount |
| Overseas Filipino worker | Latest remittance slip | Remittance record with period, sender, recipient, and amount |
| Unemployed or self-employed | Notarized affidavit explaining non-filing of ITR | Affidavit specimen with fictional declarant and income circumstances |

For elementary and JHS renewal, the later November notices explicitly ask for income evidence for **both parents**. They do not explain single-parent, deceased-parent, or guardian-only exceptions. Keep those questions unresolved rather than inventing substitute documents. A generic Certificate of Indigency is not automatically equivalent to the affidavit or residency certificate in these inspected checklists.

## Elementary — Grades 4–6

The inspected announcements cover Grades 4–6; do not extrapolate them to Kindergarten or Grades 1–3. [Grade coverage: S25-LEVEL](https://t.me/pasigscholarship/333?single), [2026 renewal: S26-CORE](https://t.me/pasigscholarship/597).

### New application

Use **N25**, with an elementary application and the child's school records. This is a **historical SY 2025–2026 fixture**. A complete new-applicant checklist for SY 2026–2027 was not verified in this research.

Suggested bundle: a fictional Grade 4 applicant, enrollment for 2025–2026, preceding school-year report card, income evidence, residency certificate, ID, photo, application, essay, and intake form. The choice of preceding report-card year is a test-data design choice; S25-REQ says latest report card without specifying a semester.

### Renewal application

Use the **later elementary-specific list**, not just the August general announcement:

1. Enrollment evidence for AY 2026–2027.
2. Report card for AY 2025–2026.
3. Both parents' income evidence.
4. Latest barangay residency certificate, stating years of stay.
5. Both sides of the school ID.
6. Application form.
7. GCash form.

Source: [S26-ELEM, 7 Nov 2026](https://t.me/pasigscholarship/651). Call this set **E26-NOV**.

The August list additionally names a white-background 1 × 1 photo. Its omission from the November caption does not prove removal: keep a photo specimen available and flag the final submission checklist for confirmation. The November notice does not expose the GCash form's fields; do not invent them.

The [August-linked elementary blank form](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1keRWKPUsSMhniWlCfNj9_pbVGFZKTlwM/view) was readable. The later [November application link](https://tinyurl.com/PCS-ELEM-RENEWAL-26-27) returned an authorization response during research, so its embedded checklist and attachments were not inspected. [S26-ELEM-SCHEDULE](https://t.me/pasigscholarship/656) specifies Google Forms for this application; the older portal instruction must not override it.

Acceptance of a particular registrar document or portal screenshot needs its own source. Keep the original requirement wording and the sample document's actual type separately.

### PAG-ASA — separate college program

Do not merge PAG-ASA with CORE. The following is a **historical Batch 2, intake SY 2024–2025** checklist, useful for testing program-specific retrieval:

| Category | Historical document requirement |
|---|---|
| Identity | Certified copy of the applicant's PSA birth certificate |
| Residence | Original barangay residency certificate with years of residence, or COMELEC voter certification |
| Income | Certified parents'/guardians' BIR-filed ITR; alternatively an affidavit explaining non-filing, or Education Unit/CSWDO certification of NHTS-PR/Listahanan inclusion |
| Admission | Evidence of admission to an eligible priority program at a partner private HEI/TVI |
| Academic record | Certified SHS report card, Form 138 |
| Conduct | Sworn good-character/no-derogatory-record certification from the graduating high school's principal or authorized representative |

For incoming second- to fourth-year nursing applicants, the historical instructions replace the SHS academic and conduct documents with certified prior tertiary grades and a dean/authorized HEI representative's conduct certification. Sources: [S24-PAGASA page 6](https://admin2022.pasigcity.gov.ph/storage/images/2024/07/22/669dabc1d139c17216091536.jpg), [page 7](https://admin2022.pasigcity.gov.ph/storage/images/2024/07/22/669dabc842ec817216091607.jpg).

The [2026 Batch 4 advisory](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/anunsyo-para-sa-pag-asa-scholarship-applicants-batch-4-only-sem-1-sy-2026-2027-850) confirms a later intake and readiness-test process. It does **not** validate carrying this 2024 list forward. A complete current PAG-ASA new/retention checklist remains unverified; retain the historical data as archived fixtures.
